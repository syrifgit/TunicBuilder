## Your three questions

- **Don't letterbox.** The tool sizes every badge from the ruler and stretches the art
  to fit, which is how the army chevrons already work. Letterboxing keeps the art's
  shape but draws the chevrons narrower than the real badge, and the footprint is what
  this tool exists to get right.
- **Corporal doesn't need a re-measure.** The army poster does the same thing. Its
  chevron art runs 0.6 to 1.0 cm taller than the real badge, worst on the shortest one,
  and we put that down to an embroidered field the finished badge doesn't have. Your
  Air numbers are 0.5 to 1.0 cm, worst on Corporal. It's the same effect, not a bad
  ruler.
- **Keep the bronze.** Poster over Blatherwick, for the reasons you gave. Thanks for
  putting the reasoning in the manifest.
